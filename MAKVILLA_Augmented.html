Dependable systems behind your work

MAKVILLA is a private company based in Ekurhuleni, Gauteng. We take on recurring work and defined projects so clients can focus on running their business or organisation.

Every engagement starts with a written scope, a named point of contact and clear approval limits. During delivery we report on progress and tell you which decisions are yours to make.

We work with SMMEs, property owners, corporate teams, nonprofits and public institutions. Remote delivery is available nationally where the scope allows.

Founder

Siyabonga Makhubu founded MAKVILLA to help entrepreneurs and organisations put reliable systems behind their work: agreeing what needs doing, documenting decisions and following through on commitments.

What we do

Three services, each run on the same pattern: agree the scope, do the work, keep records, report back. Select a service to see how it works.

How we work

Every engagement follows the same four stages, whatever the service.

Stage 1

Understand the need

We listen, ask what outcome you need and who approves the work.

Stage 2

Agree the scope

We confirm in writing what’s included, how costs are authorised and when invoices fall due.

Stage 3

Deliver the work

We do the work, keep records and keep you informed of progress and decisions.

Stage 4

Review the result

A short handover or monthly report shows what was done, and we review the account with you.

Principles we work by

We draw on the people-first service standards of Batho Pele and the ethical, accountable approach to governance in the King V Code, and apply them to how we serve clients and make decisions.

People first

We listen before setting scope, explain service standards clearly and make it easy to raise a concern.

Ethical leadership

We act with integrity, disclose conflicts and keep records so commitments and approvals can be checked.

Responsible stewardship

We use resources carefully, report on what was delivered and address problems openly.
